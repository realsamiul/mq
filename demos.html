Flood Intelligence System

Multi-Modal Disaster Response with Zero False Positives

Sylhet, Bangladesh - June 15, 2022
30 min vs 7 days traditional
91% mIoU Accuracy
15,000 km² Coverage

The Challenge

Traditional flood mapping requires 7 days of manual analysis by experts. During the critical first hours of a disaster, this delay costs lives and infrastructure.

Our Solution

Multi-Modal Data Fusion: Combined Sentinel-1 SAR (cloud-penetrating radar), Sentinel-2 optical imagery, and SRTM digital elevation models for comprehensive analysis.

Physics-Informed Pseudo-Labeling: Scientific approach combining SAR backscatter analysis (vv < 0.2 indicates water), NDWI thresholds (ndwi > 0.1), and terrain analysis (slope < 0.05).

Vision Transformer Architecture: SegFormer-B2-lite with 3-channel fused data input, trained on physics-informed labels for state-of-the-art segmentation.

Proven Results

  • Speed: 99.7% faster than manual analysis (30 minutes vs 7 days)
  • Accuracy: 91% mIoU on validation data
  • Multi-Country: Validated on Pakistan, Bangladesh, and India flood events
  • Zero False Positives: Critical for emergency response decisions
  • All-Weather: SAR penetrates clouds for day/night imaging

Business Impact

Disaster Response: Faster evacuations save lives
Insurance: Accurate damage assessment for claims
Government: Real-time intelligence for emergency services
Infrastructure: Early warning for protection measures

The Rosetta Stone Journey

Can AI discover agricultural stress patterns without human labels? Traditional crop monitoring requires extensive field surveys and expert labeling. We proved self-supervised learning can autonomously identify stress areas.

Our Innovation

Self-Supervised Learning (SimSiam): Model teaches itself healthy vegetation patterns from unlabeled satellite imagery. No human labels required - the system discovers fundamental visual features autonomously.

Unsupervised Clustering (K-Means): Automatic identification of 4 distinct landscape patterns through clustering on learned features. Cluster analysis revealed low NDVI signature indicates crop stress.

Multi-Spectral Analysis: 9-band Sentinel-2 optical imagery with NDVI, NDWI, and vegetation indices for comprehensive health assessment.

Discovery Results

  • Stressed Area: 8.27% of agricultural region automatically identified
  • Zero Labels: Completely unsupervised approach - no training data required
  • 4 Landscape Clusters: Healthy vegetation, water bodies, bare soil, stressed crops
  • High Precision: 10m pixel-level stress mapping
  • Scalable: Can analyze any agricultural region globally

Technical Breakthrough

Problem: Traditional methods require labeled training data (thousands of expert annotations)

Solution: SimSiam learns from unlabeled satellite imagery - model discovers patterns autonomously

Result: Rich feature representations without human labels - enables analysis of vast unlabeled archives

Impact: Identifies stress areas without prior knowledge of what "stress" looks like

Business Applications

The Three-Stage Journey

Stage 1: The Chimera (Deliberate Failure)

Built a "perfect" 98% R² model on simulated data to inoculate against vanity metrics. Key insight: A model 98% accurate on the wrong problem is 100% useless.

Stage 2: The Abyss (Necessary Failure)

Achieved negative R² (-0.23) on real chaotic freight data. Honest failure proved more valuable than misleading success. Scientific falsification pointed toward breakthrough.

Stage 3: The Rosetta Stone (Breakthrough)

Discovered the "Trade Imbalance Ratio" - proprietary feature measuring eastbound/westbound price difference. High ratio indicates US demand + weak return demand = capacity shortage precursor.

Proprietary Innovation

Trade Imbalance Ratio: Core alpha-generating feature
