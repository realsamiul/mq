Overview

HawkEYE represents a breakthrough in geospatial intelligence through multi-modal satellite data fusion and advanced Vision Transformers. The engine processes Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 optical imagery, and digital elevation models to deliver zero false positive disaster response.

99.7% Faster Analysis 30 min vs 7 days
95%+ Accuracy (mIoU) Flood detection
Zero Labels Required Self-supervised

Technical Innovation

  • Multi-Modal Fusion: Combines SAR, optical, and DEM data for comprehensive analysis

  • Physics-Informed Learning: Scientific approach to pseudo-labeling

  • Real-Time Processing: Automated pipeline from satellite data to actionable maps
